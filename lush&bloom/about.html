Our Story & Craft

Translating unspoken words into blooming botanical arrangements

Where Botany Meets Poetry

Lush & Bloom began with a simple passion: rediscovering the ancient Victorian language of flowers (Floriography). We believe that choosing flowers shouldn't be random—every leaf, petal, and herb carries a message of hope, gratitude, love, or healing.

Alongside our blooms, we integrate aromatic and medicinal herbs such as rosemary for remembrance, lavender for tranquility, and mint for fresh beginnings.
