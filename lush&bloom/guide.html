Florist Care Routine

Extend the vase life of your delicate ranunculus and tulips with these rules:

  1. Diagonal Stem Cut
    Trim stems at a 45-degree angle under cold water. For hollow-stemmed tulips, make a quick, clean cut.
  2. Cool Freshwater Swap
    Change the vase water every two days. Ranunculus thrive in clean, cold water to keep stems firm.
  3. Strip Lower Foliage
    Remove any olive or daisy leaves below the waterline to stop bacteria growth and keep the water clear.
  4. Keep Away from Direct Sun & Fruit
    Place arrangements away from warm drafts and ripening fruit, which releases ethylene gas that ages flowers faster.
